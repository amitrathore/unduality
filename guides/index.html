Unduality Guides · Coming soon

Bring the problem. Question what the mind says it means.

A problem arrives with a story, an owner, and a proposed solution. An Unduality Guide will help examine all three without supplying an identity, diagnosis, or conclusion.

Standards in development

The role

Not an answer-giver. A facilitator of inquiry.

Someone brings what appears to be a problem. Rather than immediately trying to solve it, a Guide helps slow the story down and examine what can actually be observed.

The central discipline is simple to state and demanding to practice:

Don’t tell someone who they are. Help them investigate what they believe themselves to be.

Working principles

How a Guide helps.

These are proposed standards. The practice will open only after its training and safety framework are defined.

01

Separate fact from addition

Distinguish what happened from memory, prediction, interpretation, and what thought says it means.

02

Expose the hidden premise

Ask precise questions that reveal assumptions a person may not notice they are making.

03

Stay with observation

Return from abstract explanation to what can be found in present experience.

04

Leave conclusions open

Do not insist on a nondual answer. Confusion, disagreement, and “I don’t know” remain valid observations.

05

Recognize the boundary

Know when inquiry should stop and when licensed clinical care or another form of support is appropriate.

Guided Sessions · Future offering

What remains when the story is examined?

Bring the problem. Examine what happened, what thought added, and whether the original problem survives the distinction. The purpose is clarity, not treatment or a promised outcome.

Register interest
  • What actually happened?
  • What is happening now?
  • What is memory or projection adding?
  • What is being resisted?
  • What exactly is threatened?
  • Does the original problem remain what it first appeared to be?

A real boundary

Clarity is not clinical care.

Unduality Guides will not present themselves as therapists or psychologists unless separately licensed and acting explicitly in that role. Guided Sessions will not diagnose or treat mental disorders, provide crisis support, or replace professional care.

Before this practice opens, Unduality intends to define:

  • training and assessment standards;
  • ethical and scope-of-practice boundaries;
  • contraindications and warning signs;
  • stop conditions and grounding protocols; and
  • clear referral paths to qualified care.
