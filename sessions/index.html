Level 1 · 50 minutes · Online video

Assisted Exploration

$249 USD per session

Co-conducted by Amit and a qualified psychologist. Both facilitators join you for a private exploration of your question or experience.

The psychologist contributes psychological perspective and support as an inquiry co-facilitator. Together, you can distinguish what happened from what interpretation adds, and examine the assumptions underneath.

Level 1 provides supported preparation for deeper inquiry with Amit. At least one completed Level 1 session is required before Level 2.

Level 2 · 50 minutes · Online video

Direct Exploration

$499 USD per session

A private exploration with Amit directly, without a psychologist. Continue examining thought, identity, and control through what you can observe in your own experience.

Build on the foundation of your assisted exploration and bring the questions that remain open. There is no prescribed conclusion and no insight you need to achieve.

Prerequisite: At least one completed Level 1 session. Moving to Level 2 is optional.

Preparation

A supported foundation.

At least one Level 1 session is required; two are recommended, and three or more are encouraged for a more supported foundation before exploring further with Amit.

Additional assisted sessions give you more time to explore with both facilitators before choosing whether to continue into deeper inquiry with Amit. You decide whether to continue; no result or progression is required.

The exploration

Observe.
Question.
Leave room.

Bring a question, an ordinary experience, or a problem you want to examine. Together, we explore:

  • What actually happened, and what can be observed now?
  • What are memory, prediction, and interpretation adding?
  • Which assumptions about “me” or control are present?
  • What remains uncertain when those assumptions are questioned?

Agreement is not the aim. Doubt, disagreement, and “I don’t know” are valid observations.

Your pace

You can pause.
You can stop.

Both levels are nonclinical inquiry. The psychologist’s participation in Level 1 supports exploration; these sessions do not provide psychotherapy, diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support, and do not replace professional care.

Keep your eyes open and proceed at your own pace. You can pause or end an exploration at any time. If you feel disoriented, stop and notice the screen, the room, and the surface supporting you. No conclusion or outcome is required.
