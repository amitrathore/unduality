## [0.1.0.0] - 2026-09-10

- Launch the Unduality open inquiry with a responsive homepage, direct-experience experiment, and clear explanations of duality and nondualism.
- Introduce the forthcoming *Unduality* book, its complete 22-dialogue working outline, and its two authors: Amit Rathore and The Psychologist.
- Introduce Unduality Guides and the future Guided Sessions offering with clear clinical and safety boundaries.
- Provide dedicated practice-safety, accessibility, privacy, and terms pages alongside a comprehensive project footer.
- Add custom-domain, sitemap, robots, favicon, web-manifest, 404, and social-sharing assets for GitHub Pages deployment.

### Changed

- Frame Unduality consistently as an open inquiry grounded in observation, comparison, and verification.
