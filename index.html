What does Unduality mean?

01

Duality

is the familiar experience of being a separate “me” looking out at a world: observer and observed, thinker and thought.

02

Nondualism

proposes that this separation may not be as fixed or fundamental as it appears.

03

Unduality

tests what the mind divides, what experience contains, and what remains uncertain.

Definitions can describe the question. Only observation can test it.

Experiment 01 · Origin of thought · About 20 seconds

Do you author
your thoughts?

Do not answer from memory. Run the practice and report only what you can observe now.

Step 1 of 4

Keep your eyes open. Do not try to stop thinking.

Simply notice the next thought that arrives.

Did you know what the thought would be before it appeared?

If you did not choose its content, in what sense did “you” think it?

No preferred answer. “I don’t know” is a valid observation.

No conclusion is required.

Notice the screen, the room, and the surface supporting you.

Practice safety

If this feels disorienting, stop. You never need to complete a practice. Unduality does not diagnose or treat mental-health conditions and should not replace professional care.

If a thought appears before “you” choose it, what else about the inner controller deserves examination?

The central question

The mind can solve almost anything. Except the problem of being you. What happens when it stops trying?

Not an answer. A question the rest of the inquiry puts to work.

Clarity,
without certainty.

See what is happening before memory, prediction, and interpretation take over.

Peace,
without denial.

Examine whether experience becomes less conflicted when it is not continually compared with how it should be.

Power,
without control.

Discover what becomes possible when action responds to reality instead of continually defending an identity.

Less energy managing “me.” More capacity to perceive, respond, relate, and create.

Two lenses · One experience

Everything you know appears in consciousness. Does consciousness appear in anything?

Psychology and direct observation can take the question a long way. Neither earns the last word.

Psychology · Working model

How does the mind build a functioning model of self and world, and why does that model matter?

Nondual inquiry · Direct observation

Can the presumed division between self and world be located in present experience?

Test thisDo not accept either claim until you look.

You have never encountered a universe outside experience. That does not prove the universe is made of consciousness. It makes the relationship between consciousness and reality impossible to ignore.

A boundary, not fine print

Some questions should not be asked carelessly.

Unduality is an educational inquiry practice. It is not psychotherapy, diagnosis, crisis support, or a substitute for professional care.

Every practice includes a way to pause, return to ordinary sensory experience, and leave without completing it. Clinical safeguards will develop alongside the method.
