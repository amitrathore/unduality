Principle · Accessibility

The inquiry should not depend on one way of sensing.

Our commitment

Unduality aims to be usable across devices, input methods, vision, hearing, motion sensitivity, and cognitive needs. Accessibility is part of how practices are designed, not a layer added afterward.

Current support

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, labels, and reading order.
  • Keyboard access with visible focus states.
  • Touch targets designed to meet a 44-pixel minimum.
  • Reduced-motion support through system preferences.
  • Light and dark themes without relying on color alone for instructions.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful visual elements.
  • User-paced practices with no countdown or forced progression.

Known limitations

The site uses externally loaded fonts, and some typographic compositions may reflow while those files load. The interactive observation field offers keyboard controls and a spoken label, but its spatial experience cannot be reproduced identically without vision.

Audio and video dialogues are not yet published. Captions, transcripts, and downloadable text should accompany them when added.

Report a barrier

If something prevents you from reading, navigating, or completing a practice, email hello@unduality.com. Include the page, device, browser, and what you were trying to do if you can. We will treat access problems as product defects.
