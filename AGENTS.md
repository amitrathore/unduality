# Project guidance

## Design system

Always read `DESIGN.md` before making visual or UI decisions. Font choices, colors, spacing, motion, accessibility, and the aesthetic direction are defined there. Do not deviate without explicit approval. In QA work, flag code that does not match `DESIGN.md`.

## Product language

Unduality is an open inquiry grounded in direct experience, not an editorial project, spiritual doctrine, therapy service, or wellness optimization program. Prefer “experiment,” “practice,” “observe,” “compare,” and “verify” over “content,” “teaching,” “journey,” and “transformation.” Never tell a visitor what they are or what they must conclude.

## Safety

Practices must be optional, user-paced, easy to exit, and paired with grounding language. Never claim to diagnose, treat, heal, or replace professional mental-health care.
